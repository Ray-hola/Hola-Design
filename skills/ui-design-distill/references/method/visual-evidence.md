# Visual evidence that changes a decision

Use only dimensions relevant to the source and requested output. Keep each rule's
scene, role and source context; this is not a shared aesthetic preset.

| Dimension | Capture a reusable relationship | Avoid an unsupported promotion |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Role, relative hierarchy, weight/spacing and actual text range; record loaded font when inspected | A declared family may fall back. A screenshot's pixel size is not a PPT point size or source font identity |
| Color | Resolved value, role, background relationship, alpha/theme and where it appears | A small accent can still be a brand signal; area alone does not decide semantic importance |
| Space | Alignment, containment, scale relationships and local exceptions at a known canvas/viewport | Repeated gaps suggest candidates, not proof of a universal 4/8px scale; preserve measured exceptions rather than rounding them away |
| Images | Content role, crop, focal placement and space needed for real text | A source photograph's subject is not automatically a required brand motif; media existence is not inspection |
| Information | What must be compared, ordered, grouped or read precisely; data units and meaningful states | Choosing a chart for its appearance can hide an inaccurate comparison; colors alone may not encode the distinction |
| Motion | Trigger, purpose, frequency, entry/exit relation and inspected timing | A still image cannot establish a transition. UI feedback, spoken-slide reveals and scroll narrative use different timing conditions |

When exact measurement is unavailable, retain proportions or an explicitly labeled
estimate. Do not invent precision. For code-based values preserve declared/used/
computed distinctions. For pixels preserve image dimensions and scaling. For PPTX
preserve its declared canvas and local/inherited geometry distinction.

In information-heavy work, retain the relationship between content and form:
PPT may emphasize a conclusion, UI may support repeated exploration, and a webpage
may explain evidence. The same chart or layout is not automatically appropriate
in all three. A valid alternative is recorded as an option, not as a source fact.

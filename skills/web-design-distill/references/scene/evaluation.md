# Website transfer test

Give the consumer its package plus new real content for a page absent from the
reference. Change information shape where useful: fewer plans, longer title, no
testimonial or different image availability. Ask it to select compatible patterns,
not to force new content into the source page's exact section sequence.

Evaluate source fidelity, information hierarchy/content fit, responsive/browser
behavior and transfer as separate axes. Check actual font loading, overflow,
control focus and content visibility at relevant sizes. A linter or palette score
cannot establish that the visitor understands the page or that conversion improves.

Inspect rendered screenshots, including lower sections. Fix the most consequential
problems together, then re-render affected layouts; when repeated attempts do not improve it,
try a different hypothesis and keep unresolved limitations explicit. If browser tools are unavailable, keep the output
and identify the skipped checks. Claimed conversion gains require actual behavioral
evidence or experiments and are not part of a screenshot judgment.

Prefer a separate consumer context or reviewer when available and authorized.
Record source access granted to that consumer. An author-generated sample is a
useful demonstration, not an independent comparison of competing skills.


For the executable review record, follow [loop-protocol.md](../loop/loop-protocol.md).
New logs use adaptive exploration without default four/two repair quotas; explicit
user limits and legacy journal limits remain applicable. Use
[type-and-creative.md](../method/type-and-creative.md) for typeface and creative comparisons.
A convention departure is evaluated by purpose and overall relationships, not
marked failed merely for differing. Record unsupported/missing evidence honestly.
